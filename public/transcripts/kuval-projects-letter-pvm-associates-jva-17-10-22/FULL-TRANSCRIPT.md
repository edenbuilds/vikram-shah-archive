At the outset, I, Mr. Ram Daryanani, the Director M/s. Kuval Projects Pvt.
Ltd. have lot of grief and regrets for the sadden demise of our close friend
and business associate, the backbone of your family Late Mr. Pascoal
Trindade who successfully and cordially carried out business activities with
us for past several years.
I, Mr. Ram Daryanani, the Director M/s. Kuval Projects Pvt. Ltd. due to the
situation arise due to the untimely sad demise of late Mr. Pascoal, I, would
like to address you as under:
1. I, Mr. Ram Daryanani and Late Mr. Pascoal, were colleagues and
associates since 1990 for the construction and development of a project
"Aldeia-de-Goa" situated at village Bambolim being developed by M/s.
Goan Real Estate Construction Pvt. Ltd.

1. In the year 2012-2013, I, Mr. Ram Daryanani was in look out for a
business proposal viz. a construction project in the vicinity of the above
referred project i.e. Bambolim and therefore approached late Mr. Pascoal
who showed the property i.e. survey No. 93/3 at village Bambolim, Goa,
held by M/s PVM Associates, an AOP of Late Mr. Pascoal Trindade, Mr.
Vikram Shah and Mr. Muralidhar Sadarangani.
2. After due discussion and negotiations held alongwith Late Mr. Pascoal,
Mr. Vikram Shah and Mr. Murlidhar Sadarangani, I, Mr. Ram Daryanani
approved the property for joint venture development upon following
representations by them and agreed for joint development of the said
property at the terms and conditions mutually agreed by and between
them. For the above purpose, the broad representations made by Late Mr.
Pascoal. Mr. Vikram Shah and Mr. Murlidhar Sadarangani are as under:
i)
ii)
That the said property was purchased vide sale deed dated
26/04/2006 by Late Mr. Pascoal Trindade in the year 2006 in the
name of Mr. Pascoal Trindade being "a Goan." with the funding
provided by Mr. Vikram Shah and Mr. Murlidhar Sadarangani and
one M/s. Loonkar Developer Pvt. Ltd.
That Late Mr. Pascoal thereafter got deleted names of certain
entities wrongly entered in the Form I & XIV of the revenue
records by filing of civil suit and obtaining decree for the deletion
of the names of the said entities.
iii) That thereafter the local villagers had filed various petitions before
the Hon'ble High Court which were defended jointly by Late Mr.
Pascoal Trindade and Mr. Vikram Shah and all the petitions were
dismissed and thereafter SLP filed by the villagers in the Hon'ble
Supreme Court also was dismissed rendering clear and marketable
title to the said property.

repeated request and plea by all concerned including the legal person
handling the matters i .e. Adv. Mr. Raunak Rao they are not responding
and/or coming forward to co-operate and to attend or to pursue the
proceedings pending before the Hon'ble Courts and also to sign various

cordial relationship for more than 30 years with Late Mr. Pascoal and had
tried several times to pursue the matter with his son Mr. Riva Trindade. I,
regret to say inspite of my repeated requests to Mrs. Albertina Trindade
and Mr. Riva Trindade have not come forward to do any act to resolve
the matters which requires urgency viz. granting the confirmation to the
identified purchaser and executing the consent terms etc. and thereby not
acting to clear the title of the said property resulting into stalling of all the
activities including the construction on the said property causing huge
losses to my company M/s. Kuval Projects Pvt. Ltd.
14. That Kuval Projects Pvt. Ltd., till date have invested substantial funds to
develop and almost 60% of the project as envisaged which is lying idle
for none of their fault thereby causing huge business losses - interest loss
- opportunity loss - loss of profit etc.
15. That it is imperative, as legal heirs of the Late Mr. Pascoal Trindade, that
Mrs. Albertina Trindade and his son Mr. Riva Trindade to come forward
to do all acts necessary to give effect to the said Joint Venture Agreement
dated 25th April 2014.
