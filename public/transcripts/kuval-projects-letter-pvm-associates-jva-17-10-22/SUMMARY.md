At the outset, I, Mr. Ram Daryanani, the Director M/s. Kuval Projects Pvt. Ltd. have lot of grief and regrets for the sadden demise of our close friend and business associate, the backbone of your family Late Mr. Pascoal Trindade who successfully and cordially carried out business activities with us for past several years.

I, Mr. Ram Daryanani, the Director M/s. Kuval Projects Pvt. Ltd. due to the situation arise due to the untimely sad demise of late Mr. Pascoal, I, would like to address you as under:

1. I, Mr. Ram Daryanani and Late Mr. Pascoal, were colleagues and associates since 1990 for the construction and development of a project “Aldeia-de-Goa” situated at village Bambolim being developed by M/s. Goan Real Estate Construction Pvt. Ltd.
