Sir/s,

1. This has reference to the ongoing settlement talks for the purpose of filing Consent Terms and obtaining a Consent Decree in Special Civil Suit No.
52/2013/A pending before the Court of Civil Judge Senior Division at Panaji, Goa.

2. As you are aware, that prior to the demise of Mr. Pascoal Trindade, an Association of Persons namely, PVM Associates was formed between late Mr. Pascoal Trindade, Mr. Vikram Shah and Mr. Murali Sadarangani, whereby the subject properties were transferred to the AOP upon terms agreed in terms of the Articles of AOP.

3. That as such the AOP is an interested party who would be affected by the decision and outcome in the ongoing proceedings i.e.Special Civil Suit No.
52/2013/A pending before the Court of Civil Judge Senior Division at Panaji, Goa. In view thereof, we have already preferred an appropriate application seeking impleadment as a party to the said proceedings by placing the relevent supporting documents on record before the Court.

---

4. On behalf of the AOP we wish to assure the Comunidade that the each and every terms agreed between the Comunidade and Mr. Pascoal Trindade shall be duly honoured by the AOP.

5. We request you to kindly permit us to intervene in the proceedings and carry forward the settlement proposal and finalise the same.

6. We solicit your co-operation in this regard.

Thanking you,
