I. PRELIMINARY OBJECTION: STATUTORY BAR UNDER SECTION 16(2) AND GROSS DELAY

## 1. The Application is Hopelessly Time-Barred:

The Respondent No. 4 submits that the Section 16 Application filed by Respondent No. 2 is a blatant attempt to derail the proceedings and is hit by the statutory bar under Section 16(2) of the Arbitration & Conciliation Act 1996.

Section 16(2) mandates that a plea that the Arbitral Tribunal lacks jurisdiction

“shall be raised not later than the submission of the statement of defence.”

## 2. Chronological Record of Participation and Acquiescence:

The record of these proceedings clearly demonstrates that Respondent No. 2 has submitted to the jurisdiction of this Tribunal for 18 months before filing the Application under Section 16 on March 14, 2026.
