WHEREAS:-
1. Kuval Projects Pvt. Ltd., is a reputed business entity engaged in the
business of infrastructure and land development.
2. That Late Mr. Pascoal Trindade and Mr. Ram Daryanani were close
associates since 1990 for construction and development of a project
"Aldeia-de-Goa" at Village Bambolim, Tiswadi Goa.
3. That in or around 2013, Kuval Projects Pvt. Ltd was approached by M/s.
PVM Associates through Late Mr. Pascoal Trindade who proposed the
property bearing Survey No. 93/3 of Village Bambolim, Goa for joint
development.
4. That after due discussion and negotiations with Late Pascoal Trindade,
Mr. Vikram Shah and Mr. Muralidhar Sadarangani, Kuval Projects
approved the said property for joint development relying upon the
representations contained in the Joint Venture Agreement dated
25.04.2014.
5. In pursuance thereof an amount of Rs. 2,21,00,000/- (Rupees Two
Crores Twenty One Lakhs Only) has been paid by us to M/s. PVM
Associates as provided in the said Agreement as and by way of non-
interest bearing refundable deposit.
6. That after certain modifications in the plans and upon obtaining
approval from the concerned authorities, the construction on the said
property commenced in the later part of the year 2015 and continued the
same till 2020 by which time 80% of the construction was completed.

7. However, thereafter vide Order dated 13.03.2020 passed by the Court of
Civil Judge Senior Division at Panaji, Goa in Special Civil Suit No.
SCS/52/2013/A, Mr. Pascoal Trindade and Mrs. Albertina Trindade
were restrained from undertaking any or all the activities on the
properties by way of Temporary Injunction. This in turn has stopped
Kuval Projects Pvt. Ltd., from continuing with the development in the
said property.
8. As per records, on the demise of Late Pascoal Trindade you i.e. Mr.
Riva Trindade and Mrs. Karita Trindade have been added as parties to
the said suit as his legal representatives/ heirs.
9. That in October 2020, M/s. PVM Associates through Mr. Vikram Shah
along with Late Mr. Pascoal Trindade had approached the
Communidade of Bambolim for the amicable settlement of all the
litigations and even finalised the draft Consent Terms.
10. That vide Letter dated 20.11.2020 addressed by Late Mr. Pascoal
Trindade and Mrs. Albertina Trindade to the Managing Committee of
the Communidade of Bambolim, a proposal of settlement was offered in
order to compromise all pending litigation and disputes with the
Communidade of Bambolim.
11. That on the basis of the representations made by Late Mr. Pascoal
Trindade to Loonkar Developers Pvt. Ltd. to our knowledge, the said
Loonkar Developers Pvt. Ltd. earmarked an area of 5,000 sq. mts., for
allotment to the Communidade of Bambolim for construction of certain
facilities to be used as a recreational and welfare centre for the villagers.

## Page 7 of 9

12. That in pursuance of the above draft consent terms were sent for the
approval from the Administrative Tribunal and thereafter the Revenue
Department. However, due to ill health of Late Pascoal Trindade in the
later part of the year 2021 and thereafter his demise, the entire process
came to a standstill.
13. It is observed by us that the settlement proposal has not been acted and
the consent terms as agreed are till date not executed.
14. On conducting inquiries with M/s/ PVM Associates, it is informed to us
that the same is on account of your non-cooperation for the closure of
the proceedings and/or to execution of the required documentation etc.,
which has resulted in delay.
15. We are concerned with the above as this jeopardises our investment for
development of the said properties.
16. That vide Letter dated 17.10.2022 addressed by us through the
undersigned Director, Mr. Ram Daryanani, we earnestly solicited your
co-operation in this regard. However, till date we have not received any
productive response.
17. Unfortunately, we are constrained to treat this as a dispute and are
compelled to invoke Clause 27 (Arbitration Clause) of the said Joint
Venture Agreement dated 25.04.2014 and seek reference of the same for
Arbitration before the Sole Arbitrator. We wish to appoint, Senior
Advocate Shri. Jayant Mulgaonkar at Panaji, Goa as the Sole Arbitrator.
