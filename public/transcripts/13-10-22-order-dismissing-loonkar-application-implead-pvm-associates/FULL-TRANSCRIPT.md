contribution by way of providing the properties for the

money.
5.
In my opinion, said Articles of Association does not
create any right, title in favour of the said Association based on

parties, then same ought to have been crystallized by entering into

and on the contrary, the defendant no. 20 desires to implead them
without showing any justification for doing the same.
7.
It appears from the conduct of the parties that they
wish to protract the present proceedings by resorting to filing
unmeritorious applications, till the Government accords them
permission to settle the matter. Hence, the cost has to be imposed
on the defendant no. 20.
