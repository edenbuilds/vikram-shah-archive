The Defendants No. 1(a), 1(b) and 2 abovenamed most
respectfully submit as under:

1. At the outset, the Defendants No. 1 and 2 submit that the
present Written Submissions are filed by these Defendants
to assist this Hon’ble Court and reserve their right to
advance oral arguments. Filing of the present Written
Submissions shall not be construed as a waiver of their right
to advance oral arguments.

2. In support of its case, and pursuant to the settlement
proposals and relief clauses added thereafter, the Plaintiff has filed Written Arguments dated 02.07.2022, marked as Exhibit D-165. However, the Plaintiff has confined its submissions only to prayer clause 'a.1', which pertains to the settlement proposal allegedly offered by the Defendants. In view of the said settlement proposal, these Defendants had earlier filed joint Written Submissions along with Defendant No. 20.

3. However, considering the Roznama dated 04.04.2026, it is evident that there is no possibility of settlement and, therefore, the present matter is required to be decided on its merits. Hence, the present Written Submissions.

4. The pivotal points for consideration which arise in the present suit are as follows:

a. It is a matter of record that the Consent Decree dated 12.11.1998 passed in Civil Suit No. 222/1994/B has neither been set aside nor recalled and, therefore, its effect continues to bind both the Plaintiff and the Defendants. As such, the present suit is clearly barred in law.
