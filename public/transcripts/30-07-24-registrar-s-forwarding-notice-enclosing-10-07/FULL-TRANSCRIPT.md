WHEREAS the above named Applicant through Advocate Kaif Noorani, has filed to this Hon'ble Court the above Application for Appointment of Arbitrator No.13/2024 for appointment of Arbitrator under Section 11 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 (copy enclosed).

AND WHEREAS the above Application for Appointment of Arbitrator No. 13/2024, came up before this Hon'ble Court (Coram: BHARAT P. DESHPANDE, J.) on 10/07/2024 and upon hearing Mr. Kaif Noorani, Advocate for the Applicant; this Hon'ble Court has passed the following order:

P.C.

1. Heard Mr. Noorani learned counsel appearing for the applicant.
2. This is an application for the appointment of arbitrator under Section 11(6) of Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
3. Vide Articles of Association executed on 01.04.2013, the parties agreed that in case of dispute arising out of the said agreement, the same shall be referred to the Arbitrator. Clause 35 of the said agreement refers to the intention of the parties to go before the Arbitrator.
4. Mr. Noorani submits that party to the first part expired and his legal heirs inherited the said property. Since, the partnership is not going forward and legal representatives are not cooperating, a notice having an arbitration clause was issued to Respondents.
