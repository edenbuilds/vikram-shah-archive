## MAY IT PLEASE THIS HON'BLE TRIBUNAL

The Respondent No. 2 herein states and submits as below:

1. Vide Order dated 07.08.2024, passed in Application for Appointment of Arbitrator No. 13/2024, this Arbitral Tribunal was appointed to decide the dispute between the parties which arises out of the Agreement styled as Articles of Association, purportedly entered between Mr. Pascoal Trindade, the Claimant and the Respondent No. 4 herein.

---

2. Pursuant to the Order dated 07.08.2024 passed in the Application for Appointment of Arbitrator No. 13/2024 and constitution of the present Arbitral Tribunal, the Claimant herein filed his Statement of Claim seeking, amongst others, the following reliefs:

a. This Tribunal be pleased to pass a judgment, order and decree directing Respondent Nos. 1 to 3 to pay the Claimant the sum of Rs. 20,00,00,000/- towards losses incurred by the Claimant;

b. This Hon'ble Tribunal be pleased to pass a judgment, order and decree appointing a Valuer as decided by this Tribunal to determine the value of the said property and assess the losses incurred by the Claimant;

c. This Hon'ble Tribunal be pleased to pass a judgment, order and decree appointing a Court Commissioner to partition the property by metes and bounds in terms of the agreed ratios found in the AOA and thereafter deduct the losses incurred by the Claimant from the capital account of the Respondent Nos. 1 to 3 and reduce the same accordingly and also adjust the profit/loss to the Claimant commensurately;

d. This Hon’ble Tribunal be pleased to pass a judgment, order and decree directing Respondent Nos. 1 to 3 to pay the Claimant the sum of Rs. 1,00,00,00,000/- towards damages, loss of opportunity to do business, loss of reputation, loss of mental peace and harassment, and towards future losses to be incurred by the Claimant.

3. This Respondent has filed the Statement of Defense and raised questions qua jurisdiction and/or arbitrability of the disputes raised in the claim and in no manner waives her right to assail the arbitrability of the dispute or the jurisdiction of this Tribunal.
