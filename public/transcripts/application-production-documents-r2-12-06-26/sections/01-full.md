      parties arising out of the Agreement styled as the Articles of

      Association, purportedly entered into between Mr. Pascoal

      Trindade, the Claimant, and Respondent No. 4 herein.

8.   The documents in question are germane to the present

     proceedings and relevant for the determination of the dispute

     at hand. It is apparent from the dates and nature of the

     transactions that these amounts were interlinked with the

     AOA relied upon by the Claimant, and it is evident that Late

     Pascoal Trindade had, in fact, paid/returned the purported

     investments.



9.   The said documents are highly relevant and material for the

     just, proper, and effective adjudication of the present

     proceedings. These documents are material and crucial, as

     they go to the root of the issues in dispute, directly

     undermining and falsifying the claims sought to be advanced

     by the Claimant in the Statement of Claim.



10. Furthermore, the said documents could not have been

     produced earlier, since these documents were brought to the

     attention and notice of this Respondent only recently.
