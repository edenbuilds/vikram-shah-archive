# BEFORE THE SOLE ARBITRATOR
# SR. ADV. NITIN N. N. P. SARDESSAI
AT PANAJI – GOA

VIKRAM SHAH ...CLAIMANT
## VERSUS

RIVA TRINDADE AND 3 ORS ...RESPONDENTS

# MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS HELD ON 13th DECEMBER AT
5.00 PM

1. Adv. Noorani present for the Claimant Mr. Vikram Shah. Adv.
Vledson Braganza appeared on behalf of Mr. Riva Trindade, Mrs.
Albertina Trindade and Mrs. Karita Trindade. Respondent No. 1, 2
& 3. Respondent No. 4 Shri. Respondent No. 4 Mr. Murlidhar
Sadarangani present via video conference.

2. Adv. Noorani on instructions states that he has received
instructions from his client to make a statement that without
prejudice to the Application for Interim Reliefs, they are agreeable
to appoint the Claimant and Respondent No. 1 as joint receivers
and the Interim Application could be disposed of in the said terms.

---

3. Adv. Braganza states that in view of the proposal of joint receivership as against claim for sole receivership in S. 17 Application, he will seek instructions from his party.

4. Mr. Sadarangani vehemently objects to the statement made by Adv. Noorani and states that the said statement should be qualified with the words of the AOA as members of PVM Associates.

5. In view of the request for further time and in consideration that the issue of receivership is not being decided today, the matter is adjourned with consent to 10.01.2026 at 11.00 am at the same venue and seat.

6. The matter stands adjourned as above.

7. Stand Over to 10th January 2026 at 11.00 am at the same venue and seat.
