## MAY IT PLEASE THIS HON’BLE TRIBUNAL

The Claimant most humbly begs to state and submit and under.

1. At the outset, it is submitted that the present Application filed by the Respondent No. 2, purportedly under Section 16 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 is wholly misconceived, untenable both in law and on facts, and is liable to be dismissed in limine.

2. It is submitted that the Application in question is nothing short of a figment of belated afterthought, and has been filed with the sole intent of derailing and delaying the present arbitral proceedings.

3. It is a matter of record that the Respondents Nos. 1, 2 and 3 had filed their Statement of Defence on 07.03.2025 without raising any objection as to the jurisdiction of this Hon’ble Tribunal on the grounds now sought to be urged in terms of the present Application. The present plea is raised for the first time at this belated stage and is clearly barred under Section 16(2) of the Act, which mandates that a plea that the Arbitral Tribunal lacks jurisdiction ought to be raised not later than the submission of the Statement of Defence.

4. The law on this issue is no longer res integra. The Hon’ble Supreme Court in M/s. Vidhyawati Construction Company v. Union of India reported (2025) 5 SCC 347 has categorically held that a jurisdictional objection not raised at the stage of filing of the Statement of Defence is deemed to have been waived and cannot be permitted to be raised at a belated stage.

5. It is submitted that conduct of Respondent No. 2 squarely attracts the doctrine of waiver and acquiescence. It is submitted that the Respondent No. 2, having participated in the present arbitral proceedings and having filed a detailed Statement of Defence on merits, cannot now be permitted to approbate and reprobate by raising a jurisdictional challenge at a belated stage. In fact, such conduct is impermissible in law and contrary to the Principles contained in Section 4 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 relating to waiver of right to object.
