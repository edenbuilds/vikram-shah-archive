 MINUTES OF PROCEEDINGS DATED 28th FEBRUARY 2026.


1.   None present in person.



2.   An email under the instructions of Respondents No. 1, 2 & 3 was

     sent by Adv. Jatin Ramaiya to this Tribunal on 27th February 2026,

     at 03.33 PM with the subject “Request for deferment of hearing in

     arbitral proceedings between Vikram Shah vs Riva Trindade and

     Ors. fixed on 28.02.2026”. Adv. Noorani on behalf of the Claimant

     Shri. Vikram Shah, under instructions, replied to the said email at

     04.40 PM objecting to further time being granted to the

     Respondent No. 1, 2 & 3. Shri. M. Sadarangani replied to the said

     email at 10.13 PM the same day, raising his objections stated

     therein, to the said request for further time by Adv. J. Ramaiya.

3.   It is in the interest of justice that some time be granted to the

     Respondent 1, 2 & 3, as a last and final opportunity. The four weeks

     time as sought by Adv. Ramaiya in his email is unjustified for the

     purpose of procuring a No Objection Certificate from the erstwhile

     advocate on record. As such only two weeks time is granted, as a

     last and final opportunity to the Respondents No. 1, 2 & 3 to do the

     needful to be represented in the present proceedings, failing which

     this Tribunal will proceed with the matter.




4.   In light of the above and to afford a last and final opportunity to the

     Respondents 1, 2 & 3 to appoint an Advocate to represent them in

     the present proceedings, 2 weeks time is thereby granted to the

     Respondents No. 1, 2 & 3.



5.   The Tribunal therefore adjourns the hearing on 14.03.2026 at

     04.00 PM. Stand Over to 14.03.2026 at 04.00 PM at the same

     venue and seat.
