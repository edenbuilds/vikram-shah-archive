3. The compensation and the consideration payable to the said

4. It is specifically agreed by the Co-Ordinator herein that they

other agencies/ persons whose assistance are required to
obtain and/or caused to be obtained the tasks envisaged
hereunder.
5. It is specifically agreed by the Owner herein that he shall pay
and/or caused to be paid any further amount over and above
Rs.3 Cr. if becomes payable to the said Mr.Madhukar
Cunconkar & Others and/or the said communidade for
settling their respective claims.
6. It is specifically agreed by and between the parties hereto that
the party of the second part shall discuss and/or negotiate
with intending party/ies either for the joint development of
the said properties and/or outright sale of the said properties
to any intending purchasers.
7. It is specifically agreed by and between the parties hereto that
they shall not do any.act, deed or thing which will affect the
title of the said properties.
8. Each Party personally guarantees the performance of their
respective covenants and obligations under these presents and
agrees to keep indemnified the Other Party against any loss
arising on account of non-performance thereof.
9. The parties hereto irrevocably agree that under no
circumstances these presents shall be terminated singularly by
one single party to these presents. Any breach of the terms on
the part of any party hereto shall result in to aggrieved party
demanding specific performance of this agreement and not the
termination of this agreement.
