25) The components noted that originally there were various names in their rights columns of these survey numbers and Pascoal Trinidade got the names deleted by filing Civil Suit No. 6/2007 and thereafter converted the land by obtaining conversion Sanad, obtained technical clearance approvals from Town and Country Planning Department and construction licenses from the village Panchayat of Curca, Bambolim and Talaulim in respect of properties surveyed under survey no. 93/2, 93/3, and 93/4 of Village Bambolim, Tiswadi Taluka also claimed that they have invested huge amount for development for which litigation has become long since the year 2009. The Communidade De Bambolim in its General Body meeting therefore came to the conclusion as follows:

1) That there are number of litigations filed by Communidade at various courts, viz Civil Suit No. 66/2009, Civil Misc. Appln No. 6/2010 in civil suit no. 222/94, special Civil suit no. 52/2013/A, 4/2013/A, LRC-19/2009 and Writ Petition No. 558/2016 against Maximo Mergulhao, Mrs. Dionisia Dias e Mergulhao, Mr. Pascoal Trinidade, M/s. Raghurai Tamba and Mrs. Loonkar Dev. Pvt. Ltd. and some of the orders which are challenged by the Mr. Pascoal Trinidade against Communidade in Civil Revision Application No. 46/2016 and 41/2018 pending before the Hon’ble High Court.

2) That the above suit and civil revision application prevailing at various Courts may lead to several years which could be subsequently challenged before the Hon’ble Supreme Court and therefore could be put to an end by the way of settlement, withdrawal, compromise by either applicants or respondents, to avoid burden on the coffers of Communidade on account of payment of professional fee of learned advocates appointed by Communidade.

3) That the Communidade has powers enlisted in article 30 (4) (g) of Code of Communidades (withdrawal and compromise Civil Suit).

4) That to take steps to compromise the suit required in the best interest of components of Communidade.

33) The Communidade after due deliberation has decided to compromise the suit considering the long litigation, complicated legal issues involved, huge legal expenses incurred by the Communidade, nature of the land as it was earlier and the claim of investment made by the defendants in the suit as well as independent claim of ownership with possession made by the defendants and disputed identity of the property.
