8. It is submitted that while Section 38(1) of the Act enables
the arbitral tribunal to fix the amount of the deposit or
supplementary deposit, as an advance for the costs referred
to in sub-section (8) of Section 31 of the Act; Section
38(2) of the Act categorically stipulates that the deposit
referred to in Section 38(1) of the Act shall be payable in
equal shares by the parties to the arbitration agreement.
9. As such, any direction that the costs and fees pertaining to
the arbitration shall be borne equally by the contesting
parties would be contrary to the mandate of the Act, in as
much as it results in a situation where in the Respondent
Nos. 1 to 3 as the widow and legal representatives of the
said Pascoal Trinidade are expected to shoulder a
disproportionate portion of the deposit for a fact that
despite the Articles of Association dated 01-04-2013 being
a tripartite agreement with Vikram Shah, Mr. Pascoal
