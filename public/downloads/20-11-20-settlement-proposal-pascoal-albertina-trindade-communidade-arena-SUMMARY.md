## From
1. Mr. Pascoal Trinidad and
2. Mrs. AlbertinaTrinidad
r/o Palbrika Mansion, Near Rosary School,
Dona Paula, Goa
Date: - 20/11/20210

To,
Managing Committee,
Communidade of Bambolim,
Having office at Bambolim,
Taluka Tiswadi- Goa.

Subject: Settlement Proposal with respect to various litigation in respect to the properties under survey no. 93/2, 93/3 and 93/4 of village Bambolim, Taluka Tiswadi, Goa.

Sir,

With reference to above and in pursuance of discussions we hereby give the proposal for the settlement as referred above as under:

I. Transfer of Settlement area admeasuring 5,000 sq. mts. in any one out of the above referred properties i.e. 93/2, 93/3, 93/4 properly demarcated and Fenced.

II. Payment of Compensation of Rs.10,00,00,000/- (Rupees Ten Crore Only) payable to Communidade of Bambolim.

III. Construction of Cultural/Sports Arena on the above mentioned 5,000 m2 area consisting of Basketball Court, Badminton Court, Volleyball Court, a Stage, and Ladies and Gents Toilets.

Subject to above compliances by us and filing of Consent Terms after obtaining due consent of various authorities in accordance with the existing rules and regulations all the disputes and litigations stands withdrawn viz: special Civil Suit No. 52/2013/A as well as in MCA No. 6/2010/B pending before Civil Judge Senior Divison at Panaji, Civil Suit No. 66/2009 pending before District Court, North Goa at Panaji and also LRC Case no. ___/2009 pending before Deputy Collector at Panaji,

We agree that the terms of settlement shall be drawn in consultation with the advocate of the Communidade and we shall bear legal expenses, filing terms in the court. We will pay the amount on the date of signing the consent terms and presentation of the same before the Court by obtaining the approval of Administrative Tribunal in Special

---

Civil suit No. 52/2013 and the Communidade shall not make any further claim against us and remaining land under survey no. 93/2, 93/3 and 93/4 of village Bambolim, Goa. The sketch/plan of the proposed construction is enclosed.

You are requested to proceed with the procedure by accepting above terms and giving letter of acceptance by the managing committee of the Communidade of Bambolim at the earliest.
Thanking You,

Yours faithfully

Mr. Paseoal Trinidad Mrs. Albertina Trinidad
